    How to play

    • Two pictures sit side by side. They look the same — but 5–7 differences are hiding.
    • Click a difference on either picture. A green circle marks it and you score 100 points.
    • Find 3 quickly in a row for a combo x2. Finish fast for a time bonus.
    • Wrong click: −5 seconds and one life. You have 3 lives and 90 seconds per level.
    • Stuck? Hint (−30 pts) pulses a circle around one difference for 2 seconds.
    • Zoom lens: hold the right mouse button on a picture to magnify it — perfect for tiny differences.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter picks, H hint, P pause, M mute.
    • Zen mode has no timer and no lives. Daily gives everyone the same scene each day.
